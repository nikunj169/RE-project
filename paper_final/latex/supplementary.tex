\section{TOST Equivalence Test Details}

\subsection{Methodology}

The TOST procedure tests whether the mean of paired differences falls within equivalence bounds $[-\delta, +\delta]$. We use per-sample absolute-error differences:
\begin{equation*}
d_i = |\text{TCO}_{2,i}^{\text{obs}} - \hat{\text{TCO}}_{2,i}^{\text{MLR}}| - |\text{TCO}_{2,i}^{\text{obs}} - \hat{\text{TCO}}_{2,i}^{\text{Quad}}|
\end{equation*}
Positive $d_i$ indicates the quadratic model is more accurate.

The hypotheses are:
\begin{align*}
H_0&: |\bar{d}| \geq \delta \quad \text{(not equivalent)} \\
H_1&: |\bar{d}| < \delta \quad \text{(equivalent)}
\end{align*}

Equivalence is concluded when both one-sided tests reject at $\alpha = 0.05$, which is equivalent to the 95\% CI for $\bar{d}$ lying entirely within $[-\delta, +\delta]$.
