# Cover Letter Draft

Dear Editor,

We submit the manuscript “How Low-Dimensional Is the S–T–AOU Dependence of Ocean Total Dissolved Inorganic Carbon? A Symbolic Regression Analysis.”

The study asks how much empirical TCO2 structure can be compressed into an interpretable nonlinear representation. We compare predefined linear, rank-1 quadratic, rank-2 quadratic, full quadratic, cubic, and symbolic-regression model families using GLODAP v2.2023 across four basins. Symbolic regression was run across four operator spaces and three seeds, with the complete candidate libraries retained.

The principal result is a constrained empirical finding: a five-fitted-parameter rank-1 quadratic captures substantial predictive structure, while adding a second quadratic direction provides little additional benefit. More flexible symbolic expressions can achieve lower RMSE, but at substantially higher expression complexity. The analysis also documents operator-space dependence, spatial/cruise generalization, AAIW error, and Southern Ocean abyssal failure.

We do not claim a fundamental ocean-carbon law or a mechanistic derivation from carbonate chemistry. We present the rank-1 form as a low-dimensional predictive projection whose usefulness and limitations depend on domain and validation regime.

The manuscript, analysis code, generated results, and reproducibility records are provided with the submission materials. Author, repository, DOI, and funding metadata will be completed in the final submission version.

Sincerely,
